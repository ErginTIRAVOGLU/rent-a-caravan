## Brand & Style

The brand personality channels maritime exploration, salt-air freedom, and the effortless serenity of driving along the sunlit cliffs of the Aegean and Turquoise Coasts. It addresses wanderers, roadtrippers, and coastal explorers seeking reliable, premium campervan experiences without corporate sterility or rugged utilitarian roughness. The interface must evoke expansive horizons, refreshing sea spray, crisp morning departures, and balmy sunset stopovers.

The design style combines **Modern Coastal Minimalism** with **Tactile Oceanic Polish**. It prioritizes breathability, generous white space, soft marine surfaces, and precise micro-interactions. Surfaces are sun-drenched and clear, anchored by heavy, nautical deep indigo typography and elevated with warm coral accents reminiscent of terracotta coastal villages and golden-hour horizon markers.

## Colors

The color system draws directly from the transition between the deep trenches of the Mediterranean and the turquoise-lit shoals of the Aegean:

- **Primary (`#16324F` - Deep Indigo Navy):** Anchors navigation, foundational typography, solid primary buttons, and key interactive focal points. Conveys structural stability and maritime heritage.
- **Secondary (`#2A6F97` - Aegean Coast Azure):** Highlights secondary calls-to-action, active status states, route lines, interactive filters, and supportive badges.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#EE6C4D` - Coral Terracotta):** Reserved for booking highlights, critical notifications, price markers, availability indicators, and joyful seasonal highlights.
- **Canvas Neutral (`#F6F9FB` - Coastal Off-White):** Provides an expansive, non-glare marine background evoking whitewashed stone and salt mist.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for elevated itinerary cards, vehicle profiles, and selection modals.
- **Typography & Boundaries:** Deep Navy Slate (`#132238`) for headlines and high-contrast copy; Coastal Slate (`#5C6E82`) for secondary metadata and vehicle specs; Sea-foam Mist Border (`#DEE6ED`) for razor-clean structural containment.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all typographical tiers to balance modern geometry with open, welcoming terminal strokes. Its wide apertures allow superior legibility in bright sunlight conditions common during coastal camper expeditions.

- Use **display-hero** and **headline-lg** for evocative campaign anchors, coastal road trip titles, and van tier categories.
- Restrict uppercase transformations solely to **label-sm** for specifications (e.g., "DIESEL MANUAL", "4 BERTH", "KAPUTAŞ ROUTE").
- Maintain tighter letter tracking on larger titles to give deep navy headlines an authoritative, premium editorial posture.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column system on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single or dual-column configuration on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous outer margins (`3rem`) to evoke broad horizon views and uncluttered navigation. Max page container is capped at 1280px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) side margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins for maximum finger-tap ergonomics while on the move.

Spacers adhere strictly to a crisp 8pt base grid (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px), maintaining predictable rhythm between vehicle specs, trip itinerary steps, and map controls.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh, generic drop shadows in favor of **Ambient Marine Shadows** and subtle atmospheric diffusion:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base canvas `#F6F9FB`. Outlined solely by subtle `#DEE6ED` dividers.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Background `#FFFFFF`, bordered by 1px solid `#DEE6ED`, layered over an ultra-soft navy-tinted ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(22, 50, 79, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating Filters):** Scaled depth utilizing dual shadows: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(22, 50, 79, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(42, 111, 151, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navbars / Booking Overlays):** Frosted maritime glass effect (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) supported by `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(22, 50, 79, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** shape vocabulary. The gentle curves reference aerodynamic camper bodies, coastal cove geometries, and smooth ocean pebbles:

- Base controls, text inputs, search fields, and buttons feature `0.5rem` (8px) border radii.
- Medium components such as van summary cards, filter drawers, and itinerary steps adopt `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Large elements including hero feature panels, imagery frames, and booking checkout sheets utilize `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Status tags, amenity pills, and route micro-badges break the hierarchy with full pill radii for clear semantic contrast against structured cards.

- Container: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DEE6ED`, radius `1rem`, elevated with Level 1 ambient navy shadow.
- Image Header: Fixed 16:10 aspect ratio with smooth top corners conforming to the card radius; holds floating pill badges for capacity and transmission.
- Content Structure: Primary title in `#132238`, specifications separated by subtle vertical `#DEE6ED` dividers, price highlighted in large indigo bold paired with coral nightly accents.

- **Amenity / Spec Chips:** Background `#F6F9FB`, border 1px solid `#DEE6ED`, text `#5C6E82`, `font-size: 13px`, full-pill border radius.

- **Van Specs Matrix:** Grid of micro-icons depicting solar capacity, clean water volume, bed layout, and clearance stats encased in `#F6F9FB` inset tiles.
